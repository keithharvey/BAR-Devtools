Adds the sharing mode presets (`modes/sharing/enabled.lua`, `disabled.lua`, `easy_tax.lua`, `customize.lua`) as `ModeConfig` tables keyed by `ModeEnums.Modes`, plus the `sharing_mode_helpers` utility that builds the unit-sharing category item lists and provides `resolveByTechLevel` for tech-tiered mod options. Introduces the economy boundary layer: `economy_waterfill_solver` (binary-search lift redistribution with per-ally-group tax via `shared_config.getTeamTaxRate`), `manual_share_ledger` (accumulates manual-share sent/received and folds them into `EconomyTeamResult[]`), and `shared_config` (cached mod-option readers for tax rate and resource-sharing-enabled). Replaces the old flat `easytax`, `tech_blocking`, `disable_unit_sharing`, and `disable_assist_ally_construction` mod options in `modoptions.lua` with a structured "Sharing" section whose keys come from `ModeEnums.ModOptions`, and adds the `ModeConfig`/`ModOptionConfig` type definitions.
